Book 3, Batch 8: Older Than the Kingdom (ch 69-76)

Monday 18 December yr19 to Saturday 26 May yr20. Eight chapters, synopsis v1, waiting on your yes. This is the quiet stretch, and the institutions do their work without the king. Elected reeves take over the small towns' watches. Keller makes a lord's captain stand down. The Seed Act passes, amended against Emeric. Ward caps the planting and wins. The batch ends at the first fruit, because summer and autumn yr20 hold locked scenes and belong to Batch 9. The year skips start after that.

Ch 69. The Long Table

Reuben Orley, a Grayrock miner who has been in a chair for nine years, asks Imani whether his legs can come back. She tells him yes, slowly, and what it will cost, and he says yes before he asks his wife. At the Long Table, Runa has made thirty-one gifts and hands them out in order of how much she likes people. Ilenne comes without a tally this year.

Ch 70. The Latch

Imani cuts the scar out of Reuben's spinal cord and builds the first span across it. It's the first spinal repair anyone has tried. Elwin Gorran gets nine months. The miller sits in the gallery and doesn't know whose money is holding his wheel. Gethin comes out of Turnstone, finds the officer who walked free lying drunk in the street, and opens the man's door latch with the fingers Imani regrew.

Ch 71. Reeves

The towns' temporary watch licences end on 23 February, two years to the day after the Arms Act. Cinderhale elects the old mill lord's cousin as its reeve, and Maudie writes the count on the wall. In Tarrow Bridge the hereditary captain refuses to hand over to the elected reeve, citing the duke's ordinance. Keller rules that a lord's ordinance can't set aside an Act of the Assembly, and the captain stands down the same afternoon.

Ch 72. Names

On Lantern Night the year's names go up with the lanterns, Gil Haverty's and Brask's three among them. Nine days later a warehouse burns in the dock quarter. The fire golems find two men alive in the smoke, and a rescue golem braces a fallen floor so they can crawl out. It's the first real rescue. Reuben's toes move, and then nothing moves for three weeks.

Ch 73. Older Than the Kingdom

The dig opens on the west hill. Ward says the one thing he knows: his master showed him the footings once and said they were older than the kingdom. Under the scrub the diggers find a road, a waystation with keyed stone in every footing, and a layer of char. Nobody can say who burned it. Keller throws out the lords' first lawsuit as premature, because a bill takes nothing until it's law.

Ch 74. Kindled

The Kindling becomes free at every Kaldo clinic, and the queue goes round the block. The Seed Act passes, amended against Emeric: the Grove Watch now answers to the Assembly's own inspector. Merryn Pellow nets a pup alive, and it goes into the research pen under seal.

Ch 75. Crystal and Fruit

In the pen, a cut made with steel closes in an hour, and a cut made with a beast's claw crystal stays open for two days. The first hundred trees drop their first fruit, about seven hundred seeds, counted under the new Act. The first glow grid spells TESS, badly.

Ch 76. Between the Bars

Emeric asks for two soak runs, and Ward holds the planting to one run because every new grove needs its guard post first. Ward wins the vote. Reuben walks the length of the parallel bars with nobody holding him while his wife counts the steps. At Wayford, Mochi plants the replacement pair in what was ash in December. A sparrow on the drop cage won't answer Runa, and won't refuse her either. Mochi won't go near it. Batch 8 ends.

Closed, and handed on

Twenty items are closed in section 6. The main ones: the licences end on 23 February, Reuben Orley is the first spinal repair, the dig's finds, the lords' lawsuit, the first Watcher, the research pen, the first rescue, the Seed Act and Ward's cap.

Batch 9 covers summer and autumn yr20 whole: Corrick and Kezia go free and Movement Seven plays out, then the Titles Act passes and the League holds its last scene. The year skips begin after it.

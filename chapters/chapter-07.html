Book 3, Batch 5: Where's My Wife (ch 38-47)

Synopsis v1, clean, awaiting your approval, not drafted. Monday 10 April to Thursday 8 June, year 19. Rules BZ and CA to CW. Movement IV and the Black Moss are placed and dated, and nothing locked is rewritten.

The spine

Imani is taken, found in a day and freed in six. The king breaks a term of the law to do it and is censured. A week later he kills five hundred men inside the law. Then the review shuts him out of the one place he fears, and the Authorisation ends because nobody needs it.

Ch 38. The East Stope

Monday 10 April. The closed face comes down, nine die and forty-one are trapped. Imani goes, though Ferra and Ward argue against it. Ward checks the three exits. Mother Brannagh wants Saint Ilda's prayer, so the relic case is weighed, sealed and sensed. Ward protests in writing, and Imani accepts the risk.

Ch 39. Release

Sister Inga's team runs crush care for all forty-one, and Imani ties the six held vessels by sight. On day 3, Friday 14 April, Cuthbert forces the stone. Imani finishes Cal's tie blind, shoots one attacker, and Sir Edwin is stabbed. She trades herself for the patients and is taken into the Coffin Level.

Ch 40. Black

The bond goes black, comes back, then thins to a thread. Ferra brings Runa. From Kestle, Kezia draws the old workings, and the drawing reaches Ward as Runa finds the Rise. At the adit there are two carriages. "A shawl isn't a person." Cal dies that night.

Ch 41. The Count

Ferra's flicker log matches the keep's rack of stones, and by the first dawn they know where Imani is. The decoy at the Holt fort is broken. From the cliff, Runa finds the window and smells the powder. Pryor's terms come at the parley, and Agatha brings a delegation to stand witness.

Ch 42. Six Days

Keller rules on the custodial guards. Tullio writes one sentence: release the next tranche only while the guest remains at the Gate. The Crestless climb is turned back by the wind and a dog. The Assembly refuses Pryor's terms. Imani's code reaches Vantic. Every flicker, Imani sends calm, and Emeric waits.

Ch 43. Where's My Wife

At 00:40 the flicker brings terror, and Runa smells the convoy waking and runs down its outrider. The king changes and roars at the gate, presses the tower men, and lets go. Matt Lowrie dies under Imani's hands. Quarrie reads her finding: "This pass binds the crown. Not only you." Davey's father hands his son the match. Imani's warning saves the pass bridge. Pryor is handed over. The bond comes back, and Runa reaches Imani first.

Ch 44. Royal Force

Quarrie's inquiry censures the gravity press, finds going early reasonable, and says Matt's case can't be known. The Rule on Royal Force is adopted on 25 April. Tullio is arrested. Malloch gets two rack stones out of the convoy's box, burns Fennick's Cross, and refuses the pardon. Ward asks, "Then what are you?" The king answers, "Standing up."

Ch 45. The Black Moss

Thursday 27 April, at dawn. Ward countersigns, the proclamation is read, and the falling star lands. About 1,300 walk away. On the count's last breath he changes down, Malloch forces the stones, and the five hundred who stayed charge. It lasts fifteen minutes. Nobody is left to heal. Imani asks him what the law gives him the right to do.

Ch 46. The Door

Armand concludes that the Church's history is a lie. The inquiry finds the Moss lawful, and says lawful isn't clean. Abroad they call him the gold-eyed demon king of Kaldo. Brega and Petrok find a door under the sand, and Brega closes it again. On day 120 the note converts at par. Petrok takes the empty chair.

Ch 47. Part Two

The king renews his request to listen to the hollow. On 26 May, in the Islanders' hall, the review seals the hollow to every crown, with two keys: one for Mother Sabbe and one for Petrok. He says once why he dislikes it, and signs. Ferra says, "That's two." Duvrenne agrees. Vantic's tower is released. On 8 June nobody moves a renewal, and at midnight the Authorisation lapses.

Book 3, Batch 7: The Year They Have (ch 58-68)

Friday 1 September to Sunday 17 December, yr19. Eleven chapters, synopsis v1, waiting on your yes. The spine is the year the hereditary council takes. Emeric wrote that year into the Charter himself to get the lords' oaths, so he can't hurry it. The Assembly renews the beast line and amends it against him again. The League votes down Corrick's boycott with neither man in the room.

Ch 58. The Bill

The day after the hanging morning, Halverne's member tables the Titles Act. The warning bands go onto the Islanders' wrists first, Mother Sabbe's people leading, and ten shore sentinels stand on the Isle's grey beaches and Kaldo's coast. Mother Sabbe holds the key to her four. At Stannary, four rescue golems walk into a staged fall and stop twice. The second stop is right. Ward writes "not yet a rescue" on the sheet, because nobody was under it.

Ch 59. Whose Coin

At the hearing, Tom Ashlock speaks for the note and against the slow country redemption, and learns that Vantic's House paid his hands in February. The Board moves every county office to weekly redemption on the bridge machine's roster and gives each office one of Ottoline's calculators. The Act passes its first vote. Tarrowmede and the March vote against.

Ch 60. The Year They Have

At Milford, the Grayrock Forge's first cold-car frames arrive, the sun house goes up, and Hester solves the meat glut without the king. The hereditary council takes its year, to 27 September yr20. Emeric asks whether the year can be shortened. Ferra asks who wrote the clause. Liora's sister writes from the isles: the elders read Liora's own letter aloud and said "He finishes things. Now we've seen what he finishes."

Ch 61. Long, Long

A young follows a shard barge into Kaldo's bay at night. The bay-mouth sentinel rings two thousand wrists. The Harbor Board fires the whirlpool mines for the first time, and they push the beast onto the shoal alive. Tess wants it kept. The Inspector of Sealing reads the nest clause as covering any living beast, the crown's included, so Ward kills it. In the morning Liora sees its gold-veined eyes in a shut case on the quay, and her hand goes to her lip.

Ch 62. Still

At Brask's harvest fair, a stall sells badly sealed jars, and the people who ate from them go still while staying awake inside. Three die at home. Emeric recognises the poison and tells the families the patients can hear every word. Kaldo's medics breathe for seven of them by hand, in shifts, for weeks. Imani can speed the nerves but can't turn weeks into days. The jar rule goes out over the Medical Pass Office's five names, not the king's.

Ch 63. The Line Renewed

The open licence worked, and the Assembly renews the beast line for one year with a report every quarter. The king asked for a standing licence to keep beasts for study. The Assembly gives him one research pen at the Claim instead, under the Inspector's seal. Corbin votes for the amendment. The Field's lamp line and tower are lit, and Sorrel walks the full two kilometres before she'll call it open.

Ch 64. Whole

Vantic sees whole outside shard selling in Velmoor's court. Saban's clerks trace it to Elwin Gorran. He's the Brask miller's brother, and the receiver of Tullio's seized book is calling the miller's loan. Saban asks whether the king could put the squirrel in a window. The answer is no, and Saban keeps his clerks. Vantic quietly buys the miller's note with her own money. Tess's floor fills with the first twelve build-out relay drums. The Assembly votes the Mage Corps into being.

Ch 65. Pity

Rowena comes for Harrold's Hollow Night. She is sworn onto the sealed list before anything else, sees the Bulwark in the bay, says "Pity. Pain teaches," and then says she wants one. Emeric decides she'll have one, after Ward, and not this year. On the night, Imani asks to enter September in Bettany's register, and three dukes ask for the band. Liora writes home that it wasn't the eyes. It was a woman with a knife and a man who held her whole face in his head.

Ch 66. The Third Day

Gil Haverty dies under a shut case. On the third day in the Claim's cold store, his body rises as a hatchling with a ring of his own brown at the rim of its eye. Ward burns it. Under Gil's bunk is the flask he dosed from in secret since May. Mochi goes flat and cold beside Gil's blood. Imani's first readers' class starts, and most of them feel nothing on the first day. Emeric asks Tess whether a valve amplifier can hear a nerve and sets one rule: the cap reads and never writes. Ada Lovell arrives.

Ch 67. The Swift

Sorrel flies the Mark 1 off the Field with the new radar watching, and names it the Swift. A week later a dead zone falls over Wayford's river close at night. The electric lamps stay lit, and the fire break saves one of the two trees. The trail leads to a cracked rack stone in the reeds. That points at the Church and proves nothing. Mochi is signed for, Ferra writes "she" over Osric's "it", and Corbin tables the Seed Act. Ferra opens a page titled "what else are they hiding".

Ch 68. Twenty-Three to Nineteen

Imani's rats confirm what Gil's death showed. Jenna Kitto opens her eyes and says she heard every morning's price of oats. Keller orders the register's dead burned with the families' leave. From Turnstone, Corrick proposes boycotting the circuit, and the League votes it down twenty-three to nineteen. Pim reads him the result. Corrick reads the tally himself and is ashamed of the resemblance. Batch 7 ends.

Closed, and handed on

The main ones: the hereditary council defined, the sentinels' grant, the grove chosen, the live young against the law, Brask, the smuggler, Gil's dosing dates, the readers' class needing a Kindling first, and Dovan kept by one vote.

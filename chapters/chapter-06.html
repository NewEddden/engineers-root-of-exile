Book 3, Batch 4: Ninety Days (ch 29-37)

Synopsis v1, clean, awaiting your approval, not drafted. Tuesday 29 February to Sunday 9 April, year 19. Rules BD to BY. Batch 3 was fixed on the way in (v1.1).

The spine

Four people die at a river chain, and a king who could end it alone has to ask the Assembly, wait three days, and then fight inside a law it wrote and under an officer it named.

Ch 29. Served

Ferra's year under censure ran out on 23 February. Five days later the new emergency procedure puts every stay on authorised spending with the committee's reviewer, Ilsa, so her pen comes back with nothing to halt. On Thursday 2 March the Inspectorate serves an order of entry on Baroness Sayer at her own weighing table. Her first order to her captains leaves room to hesitate, and a crown barge gets through the chain. On Saturday she writes the standing order: stop the commissioners by all force, fire included. The notary enters it in his register, and the courier keeps the signed original. In Cinderhale, one of Pelham's men is fined for contempt, and Tench signs Maudie's next petition without saying the word Hearth.

Ch 30. Lantern Night

Monday 6 March. Dacey writes Bryn on her lantern and tells the king one lantern each, so he should write his own. His stays blank. Runa writes Veya and Orren. The fleet crews wear Abloh's dress coats for the first time, Ferra reads the graduates by field, and Fennick's last rocket lands in the river. The commissioners' barge is tied up on the Tarrow, waiting for morning.

Ch 31. Four

Tuesday 7 March. The chain captain fires eighty yards short of the chain, and four commissioners die. Ruth Penhale's son swims away. On Wednesday Keller issues named warrants, seals the notary's register and refuses a blanket warrant.

Ch 32. Ninety Days

The king asks the emergency committee in one sentence, then sits through two days of debate in silence. The Authorisation passes on Friday night. Day 1 is Saturday 11 March and day 90 is Thursday 8 June. Ward is field command and Magistrate Ada Quarrie is the surrender officer. Force against a held place is allowed only after the proclamation has been read there. Quarrie also reviews every use of force. The king's force is under the same terms as any officer's. Tullio sends the March its quarter's pay the same day and keeps the duplicate receipt.

Ch 33. The Window Shuts

Vantic's window shuts on Tuesday 14 March, its thirtieth day, with about 81,000 Gold Rand accepted and about 20,190 drawn against the tower. The Issue Board puts the western shortfall at about 95,000. Three hundred pager bands go to duty staff. Kettrin's set rings: the doors' families name Petrok as their member, and Brega will come to see their dead.

Ch 34. The Chain

The lower town of Fairhythe opens its quay, and Ward stops men beating two porters who had already surrendered. With the proclamation read at the chain, the king changes in full, lifts the river chain off its posts and lays it on the bank, and nobody is touched. The upper town walks down, and Sayer is taken in her hall on 22 March.

Ch 35. Two Villages

The field command requisitions grain carts from Cray Bottom and Stile Green. Ferra argues against it, and Ilsa declines a fast stay. It's a defensible call, and Ferra hates it. Stannary submits. A week later the committee overturns the order, after both villages have gone short, and Reeve Bessa Lorne speaks at the hearing.

Ch 36. Dunholt

An ex-watchman brings word of two hundred crated guns and a convoy due out the dawn after next. Corrick calls the field command. The Hearth pulls every lock and nails a notice to the first crate for the carters. Kezia goes back for the waybill book and is caught at two. Corrick and Gethin surrender at dawn. Liss Whitlow reads the notice and walks her team home, and nobody drives. All three are remanded to Kestle gaol.

Ch 37. The East Stope

Wystan is charged, and Fulk Garside is gone south toward the March. Mabel burns Corrick's note unopened. Runa turns twelve on 2 April and mends a dead radio with her father. The mines inspector closes the east stope, but Treloar keeps a crew on the face. Jory Tregarth's men repair the Monk's Rise behind fresh lagging. On Sunday night the timbers talk.
